Contact support

Describe what happened, what you expected, and the steps that led to the issue. If relevant, include your Arca version and iOS version so we can understand the problem. Do not send passwords, payment card details, or Apple ID credentials.

Coin scanning and price references

Arca identifies a coin and estimates its condition from two photos. Scanning needs an internet connection because the photos are processed by AI through our server. See the Privacy Policy for the recipients and handling of those photos.

For clearer results, photograph one coin at a time with even lighting and a sharp view of both sides. If a result looks wrong, retake the photos. AI identification and condition estimates can be mistaken and are not professional authentication or grading.

Price ranges come from eBay asking prices, not completed sales. They are a reference rather than a guaranteed value. A limited number of matching listings may mean that a useful range is unavailable.

Arca Pro and billing

To recover access to an existing subscription, open Arca → Settings → Restore Purchases. To manage renewal, open iPhone Settings → your name → Subscriptions → Arca. Cancel at least 24 hours before the subscription or trial period ends to avoid the next charge. Deleting the app or its data does not cancel billing. Apple handles subscription billing and refund requests.

Delete my data

Open Arca → Settings → Delete my data and confirm Delete. This removes your collection and scan history on your device and in iCloud, and server records for this install, with the exceptions explained in the Privacy Policy. Deletion is irreversible. If you are offline, synchronization and server deletion require a later connection; the app keeps unfinished deletion pending.

Pro access survives data deletion. The server transaction document and Apple's DeviceCheck bits remain. For help with the deletion process, email playnowthisgame@gmail.com.
