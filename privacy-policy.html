Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 19, 2026 · Developer: app-n-apps

Arca is a coin scanner developed by app-n-apps. This policy explains the data used for identification, price references, subscriptions, analytics, and your collection.

Coin photos and AI identification

When you scan, two compressed photos of your selected coin, with EXIF metadata and GPS removed, go to our server, then through OpenRouter to Google Gemini / OpenAI to identify the coin and estimate its condition. On our server, photos are not stored. Provider-side retention follows the providers' policies; our API key is configured for “no training/no logging.” This server statement does not mean that photos stay on your device.

Price references

Coin attributes go through our server to the eBay Browse API to find listings, without user data. Only an aggregate is cached, without a user association.

Server records and subscriptions

Firebase (Firestore) holds an anonymous uid, counters, scan results (for one hour), Pro status, and originalTransactionId for usage limits, preventing duplicate processing, and subscriptions. Server records are retained until you use “Delete my data,” except for the one-hour scan results and the transactions/* transaction document, which remains after deletion.

Signed transaction information (JWS) is sent to our server to verify Pro access. The retained transaction fields are originalTransactionId and expiresAt.

App integrity and the free allowance

Apple DeviceCheck uses a device token and two bits held by Apple to enforce the free allowance, without a link to your uid. These bits are not reset by “Delete my data.” App Attest verifies that requests come from the authentic app.

Analytics, attribution, and crash reports

Amplitude receives and retains app events, your uid, and IDFV (the identifier for vendor) for analytics. IP addresses and city information are not collected in Amplitude.

AppsFlyer receives and retains installation and purchase information, uid, and IDFV for attribution through SKAN/AdServices, without IDFA.

Crashlytics receives and retains crash reports to help improve stability.

Apphud receives and retains purchase information to support subscriptions.

Your collection

Your collection and coin photos are stored in your own iCloud for synchronization.

Data we do not collect

Arca does not collect your name, contacts, geolocation (including GPS from EXIF and IP-based geolocation in Amplitude), IDFA, or photos other than the coins you select. Arca does not use tracking, request App Tracking Transparency (ATT) permission, or record your screen through session replay.

Delete my data

Open Arca → Settings → “Delete my data,” then choose Delete in the confirmation sheet, or Cancel to keep your data. Deletion cannot be undone. The process is:

  1. Delete your collection and scan history on this device.
  2. Sync the deletion to your iCloud. If synchronization cannot finish within 20 seconds, Arca shows “Will finish syncing when online” and keeps the deletion pending.
  3. Delete server records for this install. If offline, Arca queues this step and retries on the next launch. The old uid is removed from the transaction document's user set, but the transactions/* document remains.
  4. Reset app preferences, except onboarding progress.
  5. Sign out of the old Apphud identity and start a new anonymous uid. Arca checks current Apple subscription entitlements to preserve Pro access without opening a purchase-restoration sign-in prompt.
  6. Return to Home. Apple DeviceCheck bits remain unchanged.

For help with deletion, email playnowthisgame@gmail.com. “Delete my data” does not cancel your subscription; manage cancellation in your Apple ID subscription settings.
