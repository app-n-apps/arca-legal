Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 23, 2026 · Developer: app-n-apps

Arca is a coin scanner developed by app-n-apps. This policy explains the data used for identification, price references, subscriptions, analytics, advertising attribution, and your collection.

Coin photos and AI identification

When you scan, one or two compressed photos of your selected coin, with EXIF metadata and GPS removed, go to our server, then through OpenRouter to Google Gemini / OpenAI to identify the coin and estimate its condition. On our server, photos are not stored. Provider-side retention follows the providers' policies; we request providers that do not train on or retain your data, where OpenRouter supports this setting. Your photos therefore leave your device when you scan.

Price references

Coin attributes go through our server to the eBay Browse API to find listings, without user data. Only an aggregate is cached, without a user association.

Server records and subscriptions

Firebase (Authentication, Firestore, Cloud Functions, App Check, Crashlytics) holds an anonymous uid, counters, scan results (for one hour), Pro status, and originalTransactionId for usage limits, preventing duplicate processing, and subscriptions. The install-linked Firestore records are retained until you use “Delete my data,” except for the one-hour scan results and the transaction document described below. The anonymous Firebase Authentication record of your previous app user ID (uid) is not deleted by the button. If server deletion has not completed, the records remain.

Signed transaction information (JWS) is sent to our server to verify Pro access. The transactions/* document remains with its originalTransactionId identifier and a map of linked app user IDs and their last-seen timestamps. Successful server deletion removes your old uid from that map. The expiresAt field is stored in your entitlement record and is deleted with that record. When Pro access is refreshed after deletion, the new anonymous uid can be linked to the transaction again.

Recognition results (such as coin attributes, grade, and transcription), without photos and without a link to your uid, are cached on our server for up to 30 days under a key derived from photo hashes. “Delete my data” does not remove this cache. Server logs contain photo hashes and sizes, not the photos themselves.

App integrity and the free allowance

Apple DeviceCheck uses a device token and two bits held by Apple to enforce the free allowance, without a link to your uid. These bits are not reset by “Delete my data.” App Attest verifies that requests come from the authentic app.

Analytics, attribution, and crash reports

Amplitude receives and retains app events, your uid, and a device ID (initially IDFV, the identifier for vendor; a random ID after a data reset) for analytics. IP and location collection are disabled in the Amplitude SDK. Amplitude does not receive IDFA.

AppsFlyer receives and retains installation and purchase information and IDFV for advertising campaign measurement through SKAN/AdServices. Arca provides your app user ID (uid), IDFA, and purchase transaction identifiers to AppsFlyer only with your ATT permission. If you decline or revoke permission, Arca clears the AppsFlyer customer user ID before starting the SDK; purchase events continue without this user ID, IDFA, or transaction identifiers. AppsFlyer determines approximate location (country/city) from the IP address. AppsFlyer may also receive technical device and app performance information, such as app launch data, as part of its SDK operation.

Arca stores a local queue of purchase events waiting to be sent, including the StoreKit transaction identifier, app user ID, product, amount, currency, and trial status. After an event is sent, Arca keeps only the last 1,024 transaction identifiers locally to prevent duplicates. “Delete my data” clears this queue and local transaction history; it does not delete purchase data already held by Apple or SDK providers.

Crashlytics receives and retains crash reports and related diagnostic information (such as device model, iOS version, and app version) linked to your app user ID (uid) to help improve stability.

Apphud receives and retains purchase and subscription information linked to your app user ID and IDFV to support subscriptions, and IDFA only with ATT permission. The Apphud–AppsFlyer attribution integration is limited to users who allow tracking. Arca sends AppsFlyer conversion data and the AppsFlyer ID to Apphud only with ATT permission. Revoking permission stops new transfers of these data from Arca; it does not delete data already sent.

Our server (Firebase) and SDK providers receive your device's IP address as part of network requests. AppsFlyer determines approximate location (country/city) from the IP address. IP and location collection are disabled in the Amplitude SDK.

Your collection

Your collection and coin photos are stored in your own iCloud for synchronization.

Data we do not collect

Arca does not collect your name, contacts, GPS location (including GPS from EXIF), or photos other than the coins you select, and does not record your screen through session replay. Arca requests App Tracking Transparency (ATT) permission using the system prompt. If you allow tracking, your app user ID (uid), IDFA, and purchase transaction identifiers are shared with AppsFlyer to measure advertising campaigns. If you decline or revoke permission, Arca does not provide your app user ID, IDFA, or purchase transaction identifiers to AppsFlyer; purchase events continue without these identifiers. AppsFlyer conversion data and the AppsFlyer ID are not newly sent from Arca to Apphud without permission. All app features remain available when you decline.

Delete my data

Open Arca → Settings → “Delete my data,” then choose Delete in the confirmation sheet, or Cancel to keep your data. Deletion cannot be undone. The process is:

  1. Delete your collection and scan history on this device.
  2. Sync the deletion to your iCloud. If synchronization cannot finish within 20 seconds, Arca shows “Will finish syncing when online” and keeps the deletion pending.
  3. Delete server records for this install, including the anonymous Firebase Authentication record of your previous app user ID (uid). If you are offline, Arca retries when you open the app or return to it with a connection; until then the server records remain. When server deletion succeeds, the old uid is removed from the transaction document's user set, but the transactions/* document remains.
  4. Clear the local purchase-event queue and transaction history, and reset app preferences, except onboarding progress. “Coin of the day” notifications are turned off and already scheduled ones are removed; you can turn them on again in Settings.
  5. After server deletion succeeds, sign out of the old Apphud identity, clear the old uid from analytics and crash reporting on this device, and start a new anonymous uid. Arca checks current Apple subscription entitlements to preserve Pro access without opening a purchase-restoration sign-in prompt.
  6. Return to Home. Apple DeviceCheck bits remain unchanged.

The button deletes the Arca server records for this install described above when server deletion succeeds. It does not delete data already sent to Amplitude, AppsFlyer, Crashlytics, Apphud, or the AI providers (OpenRouter, Google Gemini / OpenAI); they retain data under their own policies. To request deletion of AppsFlyer data, contact Support as described below; revoking ATT does not delete prior data. The recognition cache, transaction document, and Apple DeviceCheck bits also remain.

For help with deletion, email playnowthisgame@gmail.com. “Delete my data” does not cancel your subscription; manage cancellation in your Apple ID subscription settings.

Analytics and attribution data (events, uid, and device IDs including IDFV) are stored by Amplitude and AppsFlyer as described above. AppsFlyer receives your app user ID, IDFA, and purchase transaction identifiers from Arca only if you permit tracking. The “Delete my data” button does not delete these data. To request their deletion, email Support at playnowthisgame@gmail.com with the subject “Delete analytics data” and include your app user ID from Settings, if it is shown. Send this request before you use “Delete my data”: after deletion Arca starts a new app user ID, and the old one is no longer shown. We will forward your deletion request to Amplitude and AppsFlyer. Revoking ATT permission stops future sharing of your app user ID, IDFA, and purchase transaction identifiers from Arca to AppsFlyer, and AppsFlyer conversion data and the AppsFlyer ID from Arca to Apphud. It does not delete previously sent data.

Permissions

You can revoke camera, photo library, and notification permissions in iOS Settings. Open Settings, find Arca, and change the relevant permissions; notification controls are also available under Notifications → Arca. You can change your ATT choice in Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking.

Children

Arca is rated 4+. The app is not intended to collect data from children.
